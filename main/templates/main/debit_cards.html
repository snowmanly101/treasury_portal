How to Apply for a Debit Card

  1. Ensure Account Verification: Your secure treasury account must be fully onboarded and active.
  2. Navigate to Card Services: Log into your dashboard and select the Card Services module from the main menu.
  3. Select Card Tier: Review the Terms of Service and select either the Obsidian or Titanium card option.
  4. Compliance Approval: Your application will be submitted for review by the compliance department. This process is typically completed within 1 business day for existing high-net-worth clients.
  5. Secure Dispatch: Upon approval, your physical encrypted card will be manufactured and dispatched via courier (FedEx/DHL) to your registered address. Expedited global delivery is standard.
